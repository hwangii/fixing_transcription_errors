# Jaro-Winkler similarity of 1940 transcriptions to FamilySearch profile names

Generated by MATCHING/profile_names_jw.do. Do not edit by hand.

Mean Jaro-Winkler similarity (0 to 1; jarowinkler, prefix weight 0.1) between each source's name and the FamilySearch profile's, after the normalisation of MATCHING/profile_names_score.do. Sample: 1940 records in the 49 model states with all four human name fields present, attached one-to-one to a profile whose gender does not contradict the census sex. Men: profile has a usable first name and surname, scored on both (the 'first name and surname' sample of profile_names.md). Women: profile has a usable first name, scored on first names only, since a woman's profile surname may be her maiden or married name ('All women: first name'). The exact-agreement shares of profile_names.md are reproduced here as a check. Full name = mean of the first-name and surname similarities. A name the source does not have scores 0. Ours = the model's first name (namefrst_ml) and its surname with ditto marks resolved to the surname they refer to (namelast_ml_ditto), as in profile_names.md; no fallback to Ancestry. Ours+Anc = ours with Ancestry's name wherever the model field is empty, field by field, as the linkage tables' New transcription; group 4 (missing) refers to the model names alone. Agreement with a selected, FamilySearch-linked reference, not accuracy. Cell 0 = Ancestry and FamilySearch agree; 1-4 = they disagree and our name matches Ancestry's (1), FamilySearch's (2), neither (3), or is missing (4).
