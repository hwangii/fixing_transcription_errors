### 1. Sections 1 and 3 score the linking baseline after stripping 'InvalidPred', assuming it falls back to Ancestry when the linking code actually links on 'invalidpred'

`sentinel-treated-as-empty-in-baseline-scoring` · **MAJOR** · correctness · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:96-97, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:158-166, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:192-198, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:213-216`

**What the code does**  
Lines 96-97 convert `"InvalidPred"` to `""` in `namefrst_u` and `namelast_u` (`gen namefrst_u = cond(ip_f, "", namefrst_ml)` and `gen namelast_u = cond(ip_ld, "", namelast_ml_ditto)`). Sections 1 and 3 then use `namefrst_u` and `namelast_u` to represent the linking baseline: line 213 sets `gen namelast_base = cond(namelast_u=="", namelast, namelast_u)`, lines 192-198 define `nt_f_e`, `nt_f_w`, `nt_`x'_l_e`, `nt_`x'_l_w`, and `nt_diff` from `namefrst_u` and `namelast_u`, and lines 158-166 score `tgt_de` (which includes `namelast_ml_ditto=="InvalidPred"`) against `namelast` as the baseline. However, as lines 25-26 acknowledge, the actual linking script (`MATCHING/1_divide_and_clean_everyoneV5_women_addedV2.do:169-170`) checks `if namefrst_true_cleaned==""` and `if namelast_true_cleaned==""` on the raw fields and does not fall back to Ancestry when a field is `"InvalidPred"`.

**Why it matters**  
On non-target records (Section 3), every occurrence of `"InvalidPred"` in `namefrst_ml` or `namelast_ml_ditto` is counted in `MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md:77-84` as "empty" (assumed to fall back harmlessly to Ancestry = the agreed human name) rather than "present and different", understating `nt_f_w`, `nt_d_l_w`, `nt_nd_l_w`, and `nt_diff` ("Linked name changes on either field (the erosion)") and crediting `"InvalidPred"` rows in `namelast_base` with already agreeing with `namelast`. On target records (Section 1), ditto rows with `namelast_ml_ditto=="InvalidPred"` actually have `"invalidpred"` rather than Ancestry's `namelast` as their current baseline surname.

**How to check**  
In wy/merged_data.dta, run: `count if nt & (namefrst_ml=="InvalidPred" | namelast_ml_ditto=="InvalidPred")` and `count if tgt & non_ditto_ml==0 & namelast_ml_ditto=="InvalidPred"` to see how many non-target and target records carry the literal "InvalidPred" into the actual linking baseline.

**Suggested fix**  
Either (a) update MATCHING/1_divide_and_clean_everyoneV5_women_addedV2.do (and downstream divided files) to treat "InvalidPred" as empty before falling back to Ancestry so the linking pipeline matches namefrst_u/namelast_u, or (b) define the current linking baseline in Sections 1 and 3 from the raw fields (`gen namelast_base = cond(namelast_ml_ditto=="", namelast, namelast_ml_ditto)`) and count `"InvalidPred"` on non-target records as present-and-different erosion rather than as empty fields that fall back to Ancestry.

### 2. Treating row-1 model dittos with non-empty namelast_u as chain heads (`head_r1`) severs cross-image ditto chains and forces `head_agree = 0` on all 1,919 such chains in Wyoming

`head-r1-breaks-cross-image-chains-and-zeros-head-agree` · **MAJOR** · correctness · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:115-125, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:133-144, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:214-216`

**What the code does**  
Line 115 defines `gen byte head_r1 = d & link & row==1 & namelast_u!=""` and line 116 defines `gen byte head = !(d & link) | head_r1`. Line 123 then defines `by chain: gen byte head_nd = nd[1]` and line 125 defines `gen byte head_agree = head_nd & head_anc!="" & head_anc==head_fs`. Because `row==1` is the first line of an image, there is no line above it on its own image; and as shown in `MATCHING/results/nomodel_diag.md:36,52`, raw `namelast_ml` is non-empty on 100% of `non_ditto_ml==0` rows. On 1,919 linked images in Wyoming (`MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md:22`), `row==1` has `non_ditto_ml==0` and a non-empty `namelast_u`, so `head_r1==1` starts a new chain at `row==1` whose head has `d==1` and `nd==0`. Consequently, `head_nd` is `0` and `head_agree` is forced to `0` for every row in all 1,919 `head_r1` chains.

**Why it matters**  
First, in Section 3 (`MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:214-216`), forcing `head_agree = 0` whenever a chain starts with `head_r1` silently excludes every non-target model-ditto row in those 1,919 top-of-image ditto chains from the household-consistency rule (`nt_d_hagree`, `nt_d_in`, `nt_d_out`); in `MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md:99`, 2,596 of 98,126 non-target model-ditto rows fail `head_agree` despite 100% own-human agreement, largely because `head_r1` chains have `head_nd == 0`. Second, any empty/sentinel ditto row under a `head_r1` head is excluded from both `namelast_h` (line 144) and `namelast_x` (line 142) and falls into `de_headother`.

**How to check**  
In wy/merged_data.dta, inspect row-1 model dittos: `count if row==1 & non_ditto_ml==0 & link==1`, check how often `namelast_ml_ditto == namelast_ml` vs. the previous image's row-40 `namelast_ml_ditto`, and run `count if nt_d & !head_nd` to see how many of the 2,596 excluded non-target model-ditto rows in wy (`98,126 - 95,530`) are excluded solely because their chain head is `head_r1`.

**Suggested fix**  
Verify whether `namelast_ml_ditto` on `row==1` when `non_ditto_ml==0` is merely an un-resolved echo of `row==1`'s raw `namelast_ml` (because within-image ditto resolution has no line above `row==1`). If so, do not sever the chain at `head_r1` when testing cross-image carries or household-head consistency. If `head_r1` is intentionally treated as an own-image head, do not gate `head_agree` on `head_nd == 1` (e.g., use `(head_nd | head_r1[1]) & head_anc!="" & head_anc==head_fs`, or report `head_r1`-headed chains separately).

### 3. `de_headother` is labeled 'head is a link break or a missing flag' but captures same-image and `head_r1` sentinel ditto rows whose chain head has a usable model surname

`de-headother-mislabelled-and-omits-same-image-sentinel-fills` · **minor** · claims-vs-code · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:133-142, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:296, MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md:25-30`

**What the code does**  
Lines 133-136 define `de_headempty = de & head_nd & head_ld==""`, `de_headcross = de & head_nd & head_ld!="" & head_cross`, and `de_headother = de & !de_headempty & !de_headcross`, and line 296 labels `de_headother` as `"... other (head is a link break or a missing flag)"`. By boolean algebra, `de & !de_headempty & !de_headcross` equals `(de & !head_nd) | (de & head_nd & head_ld!="" & !head_cross)`, which includes (1) ditto rows on the same image (`!head_cross`) as a non-ditto head with a valid model surname (`head_nd==1 & head_ld!=""`) whose own `namelast_ml_ditto` was `"InvalidPred"`, and (2) ditto rows under a linked `head_r1` head (`head_nd==0 & link[1]==1`). In Wyoming (`MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md:25-29,85,109-110`), `non_ditto_ml` is never missing, there are only 17 unlinked ditto heads (`117,334 - 115,398 - 1,919 = 17`), and all 121 rows in `de_headother` come from the 209 `"InvalidPred"` ditto rows (`348 ip_ld - 139 nd_ip - 88 de_headempty = 121`).

**Why it matters**  
The Section 0 table label (`"... other (head is a link break or a missing flag)"`) misdescribes the 121 rows in `de_headother`, and `namelast_x` (`replace namelast_x = head_ld if de_headcross`, line 142) leaves `namelast_x` empty on same-image ditto rows where stripping `"InvalidPred"` made `namelast_u` empty even though their chain head on the same image has a usable model surname `head_ld != ""`.

**How to check**  
In wy/merged_data.dta, run `tab head_nd head_cross if de_headother, missing` and `count if de_headother & head_ld!=""` on the 121 rows where `de_headother == 1`.

**Suggested fix**  
Explicitly separate `de & head_nd & head_ld!="" & !head_cross` (same-image ditto rows with empty/sentinel `namelast_u` under a non-empty non-ditto head) and `de & head_r1[1]` from true link-break / missing-flag heads (`de & !link[1]` or `de & !d[1] & !nd[1]`), and allow `namelast_x` to fill `head_ld` whenever `de & head_ld!=""` (or document why same-image sentinel ditto rows under a valid head are excluded).

### 4. Proxy split of differing non-target ditto surnames (`nt_dw_inh` vs `nt_dw_mis`) compares only to the line immediately above (`_n-1`), misclassifying family members below a missed surname change as 'difference comes from the head'

`proxy-inherited-split-confounds-missed-household-boundaries` · **minor** · identification · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:199-207, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:400-410, MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md:87-94`

**What the code does**  
Lines 204-207 define `gen prev_namelast = namelast[_n-1] if link`, `gen byte nt_dw_inh = nt_d_l_w & prev_namelast!="" & namelast==prev_namelast`, and `gen byte nt_dw_mis = nt_d_l_w & prev_namelast!="" & namelast!=prev_namelast`. Lines 199-201 and 403 interpret `nt_dw_inh` as `"Inherited: human surname equals the human surname above"` (`"SO THE HUMANS READ IT AS A DITTO AND THE DIFFERENCE COMES FROM THE HEAD"`).

**Why it matters**  
When the model fails to detect a new surname at line $r$ (`non_ditto_ml == 0` on a new household head/boarder whose human surname changes), only line $r$ itself has `namelast != prev_namelast` (`nt_dw_mis == 1`). Every subsequent member of that new surname group on lines $r+1, r+2, \dots$ has the same human surname as line $r$ (`namelast == prev_namelast`) while still sitting in the model's unbroken ditto chain under the earlier household's head (`namelast != head_anc`). Counting lines $r+1, r+2, \dots$ in `nt_dw_inh` attributes their error to a misread chain head rather than to the missed non-ditto boundary at line $r$.

**How to check**  
In wy/merged_data.dta, run `count if nt_dw_inh & head_anc!="" & namelast!=head_anc` to see how many of the 4,261 rows classified as 'Inherited' in Wyoming actually have a different human surname from their chain head `head_anc`.

**Suggested fix**  
Use `head_anc` (already constructed at line 121) alongside `prev_namelast` (or the human `non_ditto` variable in `merged_data.dta`) to partition `nt_d_l_w` into: (1) `namelast == head_anc` (row shares the chain head's human surname, so the error is in the head's model transcription), (2) `namelast != prev_namelast` (the row itself is a human surname change missed by `non_ditto_ml == 0`), and (3) `namelast == prev_namelast & namelast != head_anc` (the row is a ditto under an earlier missed surname change within the same model chain).
