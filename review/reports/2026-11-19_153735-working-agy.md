### 2. Recoding incnonwg==1 to missing leaves incnonwg==2 as the only non-missing value, forcing its within-group share to 100.0% in every column

`incnonwg-recode-collapses-share-to-100pct` · **BLOCKER** · correctness · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/linked_groups_socio.do:52, MATCHING/linked_groups_socio.do:74-86`

**What the code does**  
Line 52 copies `recode incnonwg (1 9=.)` from `CODE/create_figures_SSHA2025.do:351`, and lines 76-85 compute each category's share within link-status group `g` as `100 * count(grp==g & incnonwg==val) / count(grp==g & !missing(incnonwg))`. In IPUMS 1940 (`CODE/usa00647_for_SSHA2025_socio_demo_char.do:3764-3768`), `incnonwg` is coded `0` (N/A), `1` (<$50), `2` ($50+), and `9` (Missing). In Figure 4 of `create_figures_SSHA2025.do`, recoding `1` to `.` worked because that script computed linkage rates *within* each category (`sum linked_before if incnonwg==2`). Here, recoding `1` to `.` leaves `2` as the only non-missing value of `incnonwg` in `temp_tgt.dta` (`MATCHING/_figs_tgt_run.log:1052-1053,1138`), so the numerator (`grp==g & incnonwg==2`) equals the denominator (`grp==g & !missing(incnonwg)`) for every group `g=1..4`.

**Why it matters**  
The `Nonwage inc.>50` row in `MATCHING/results/linked_groups_socio.md` will report `100.0 | 100.0 | 100.0 | 100.0` across all four link-status groups (`Both`, `After only`, `Neither`, `Before only`) instead of the true share of each group with nonwage income over $50 (~26-28%).

**How to check**  
Check CODE/usa00647_for_SSHA2025_socio_demo_char.do:3764-3768 and MATCHING/_figs_tgt_run.log:1052-1053,1138: `recode incnonwg (1 9=.)` makes 37,609,917 changes and leaves only `incnonwg==2` (13,426,942 records; 37,609,917 + 13,426,942 = 51,036,859) as non-missing.

**Suggested fix**  
Change MATCHING/linked_groups_socio.do:52 from `recode incnonwg (1 9=.)` to `recode incnonwg (0 9=.)` so that `incnonwg==1` ("Less than $50 nonwage, nonsalary income") remains in the non-missing denominator `d1..d4`. Then either add a label for `v1=="incnonwg" & v2==1` (e.g., `"Nonwage inc.<50"`) or `drop if v1=="incnonwg" & v2==1` after `postclose` at line 88 if only the `Nonwage inc.>50` row is desired.
