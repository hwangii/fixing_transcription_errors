### 1. First-name sentinel fallback to Ancestry leaves mi_true_cleaned empty, excluding all newly linked sf==1 records from own-MI validation

`first-name-sentinel-fallback-omits-ancestry-mi-from-own-mi-validation` · **minor** · claims-vs-code · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/1e_tgtns_divided_files.do:19-23, MATCHING/1e_tgtns_divided_files.do:101-110, MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:126-130, MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:231-245, MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:321-338`

**What the code does**  
In MATCHING/1e_tgtns_divided_files.do:101-110, records with `f_name=="invalidpred"` (`sf==1`) have `f_name` replaced by Ancestry's `f_ans` (`replace f_name = f_ans if sf`), while `mi_true_cleaned` is left at `""` (`assert mi_true_cleaned=="" if sf`) to mimic `1_divide_and_clean_everyoneV5_women_addedV2.do:169-173` (which ran `abeclean` a second time on already-stripped `namefrst_ans_cleaned`). In MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:232-238, `Share validated, MI (%)` (`sv`) and `Links with both initials` (`nv`) require `mi1930 != "" & mi1940 != ""` using `mi_v_mltgtns` (`mi_true_cleaned`), while `nva` (the denominator of the fixed-yardstick `Share validated, Ancestry 1940 MI (%)` row computed at line 243) is omitted from the markdown output loop at line 333.

**Why it matters**  
Because a 1940 record with `f_name=="invalidpred"` could never match any 1930 Ancestry record in `mltgt`, every link gained in `mltgtns` from fixing a first-name sentinel (`sf==1`) uses Ancestry's 1940 first name `f_ans` yet has `mi1940==""` in `mi_v_mltgtns`. Consequently, 100% of links recovered by the first-name sentinel fix are excluded from `Links with both initials` (`nv`) and `Share validated, MI (%)` (`sv`), even when Ancestry recorded a 1940 middle initial (`mi_ans!=""`). They enter only `Share validated, Ancestry 1940 MI (%)` (`sva`), whose denominator `nva` is not displayed in `MATCHING/results/tgt_linkage_abe.md`.

**How to check**  
In MATCHING/1e_tgtns_divided_files.do, count `sf & mi_ans!=""` inside the birthplace loop, and in MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do print `nva_restr_mltgt` vs. `nva_restr_mltgtns` alongside `nv_restr_mltgt` vs. `nv_restr_mltgtns`.

**Suggested fix**  
Either print `nva` (`Links with both initials, Ancestry 1940 MI`) in MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:333-350 and note in the table caption at line 322 that target records falling back to Ancestry's first name (empty or `InvalidPred`) carry an empty `mi_true_cleaned` and are therefore evaluated only in the Ancestry-MI validation row (`sva`), or additionally report a variant where `mi_true_cleaned` falls back to `mi_ans` whenever `f_name` falls back to `f_ans`.

### 2. Sentinel removal is applied only to Target-only (mltgtns), leaving New (true/ml) confounded by non-target InvalidPred failures

`asymmetric-sentinel-removal-across-all-record-and-target-only-columns` · **minor** · identification · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:43-48, MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:321, MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:332, MATCHING/1e_tgtns_divided_files.do:11-14`

**What the code does**  
MATCHING/1e_tgtns_divided_files.do builds `divided_file1940_tgtns_cleaned` from `divided_file1940_tgt_cleaned`, and MATCHING/score_tgt_links.do:48,332 adds `Target-only, no sentinel` (`mltgtns`) alongside `Old` (`ans`), `New (paper)` (`true`), `New (rerun)` (`ml`), and `Target-only` (`mltgt`), without a corresponding sentinel-free all-records version of `New`.

**Why it matters**  
According to MATCHING/results/ditto_diag.md:96-100, roughly half of the ~600,000 `"InvalidPred"` occurrences in `namefrst_ml` and `namelast_ml_ditto` fall on non-target records where Ancestry and FamilySearch agree. In `New (paper)` and `New (rerun)`, `1_divide_and_clean_everyoneV5_women_addedV2.do:169-170` failed to fall back to Ancestry on those rows, overwriting agreed human names with `"invalidpred"` so they could never match 1930 Ancestry records. Comparing `New` against `Target-only` or `Target-only, no sentinel` therefore conflates genuine model transcription errors on non-target records with the `"InvalidPred"` fallback bug on non-target records.

**How to check**  
Compare the total raw `InvalidPred` counts in MATCHING/results/ditto_diag.md:96-100 (`namefrst_ml == InvalidPred`: 336,679; `namelast_ml_ditto == InvalidPred`: 271,055) with the target-only subset (`Target records with it in namefrst_ml or namelast_ml_ditto`: 298,969), confirming that roughly half of sentinel occurrences sit outside the target group and remain as literal `"invalidpred"` in the `true` and `ml` columns of `score_tgt_links.do`.

**Suggested fix**  
If `MATCHING/results/tgt_linkage_abe.md` is used to quantify the linkage cost of applying model predictions to non-target records, either note explicitly in the table text that `New (paper)` and `New (rerun)` still retain `"invalidpred"` on non-target (and target) records, or add a sentinel-free all-records column (`mlns`, replacing `"invalidpred"` with `f_ans`/`l_ans` in `divided_file1940_true_cleaned`) to separate the non-target sentinel bug from genuine non-target model disagreement.
