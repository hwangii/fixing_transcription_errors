## Findings

### 1. Permitted fallback discrepancies can make linkage runs differ for reasons other than model-name targeting

`fallback-tolerance-confounds-linkage-contrast` · **minor** · identification · confidence: **likely** (implied, depends on unseen data)

**Where:** `MATCHING/1c_tgt_divided_files.do:105-115, MATCHING/1c_tgt_divided_files.do:128-136, review/briefs/2026-11-17-tgt-check-nonfatal.md:3-5`

**What the code does**  
The code accumulates fallback discrepancies where name_true_cleaned_exists==0 & !(f_name==f_ans & l_name==l_ans) into nbad, but replaces the previous fatal assertion exit with a non-fatal threshold check: if `nbad' > 0.001*`nchk' exit 459. For the observed 436 discrepancies out of 1,031,654 checked records, the script logs a NOTE and continues. For any of these 436 records that are non-targets (!tgt), lines 111-114 overwrite f_name and l_name with _ans_cleaned values, while the all-records baseline (_true_cleaned) keeps the differing fallback names. Conversely, for any of the 436 that are targets (tgt), lines 111-114 do not run, leaving them with _true_cleaned fallback names rather than _ans_cleaned names.

**Why it matters**  
The explicit purpose of generating _tgt_cleaned alongside _true_cleaned in MATCHING/_tgt_only_run.do:8-9 is so that the two runs 'differ only in which 1940 records get model names'. When non-target records have differing Ancestry fallback names between the two runs, any observed difference in links or match rates between target-only and all-records is partly attributable to discrepancies in fallback cleaning rather than pure model targeting. In addition, because ABE linkage requires candidate uniqueness within age/birthplace cells, changes in candidate names can spill over to competing potential matches. Although 436 records is only ~0.042% of checked records, tolerating them without classifying them by tgt leaves the counterfactual contrast slightly confounded.

**How to check**  
In 1c_tgt_divided_files.do before line 111, run: count if name_true_cleaned_exists==0 & !(f_name==f_ans & l_name==l_ans) & !tgt and tabulate tgt among the discrepant records. After running MATCHING/_tgt_only_run.do, merge link1930_1940_mltgt1940_cleaned_noraceblock0.dta and link1930_1940_ml1940_cleaned_noraceblock0.dta by ID to verify whether any linked pair, or non-unique candidate pair, involves these 436 IDs.

**Suggested fix**  
Reconcile the upstream cleaning discrepancy between divided_file1940_true_cleaned and divided_file1940_ans_cleaned so nbad==0 before producing final links. If running temporarily, verify how many of the 436 records are non-targets vs targets, and confirm that none of them alter linked pairs or candidate competition in the resulting link datasets.
