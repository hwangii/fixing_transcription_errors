## Findings

### 1. Proxy split labels non-target ditto differences as "the head was misread" without requiring human agreement at the head, and `nt_dw_chg` omits `head_anc!=""`

`proxy-split-head-misread-conflates-discordant-heads` · **minor** · claims-vs-code · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:203-206, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:386-390, MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md:79-90`

**What the code does**  
Line 203 defines `gen byte nt_dw_head = nt_d_l_w & head_anc!="" & namelast==head_anc` and line 386 labels it "Row's human surname equals its chain head's (the head was misread)". Unlike the household-consistency rule immediately below (`nt_d_in` on line 214, which is equivalent to `nt_d_l_w & head_agree & namelast==head_anc`), `nt_dw_head` does not require `head_agree` (`head_nd & head_anc!="" & head_anc==head_fs`). When the chain head is itself a discordant/target record (`head_anc != head_fs`) and the model read the head's surname as `head_fs` (while the ditto row below is non-target with `namelast == fs_namelast == head_anc`), or when `!head_nd`, `nt_dw_head` counts the head as "misread" by the model. In addition, line 204 defines `gen byte nt_dw_chg = nt_d_l_w & !nt_dw_head & prev_namelast!="" & namelast!=prev_namelast` without `& head_anc!=""` (unlike `nt_dw_below` on line 205), even though line 389 labels `nt_dw_unk` as "Unknown: line above not linked, or no Ancestry surname above or at the head".

**Why it matters**  
In the Wyoming smoke run alone (`MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md:79, 89`), `nt_dw_head` is 4,169 whereas `nt_d_in` ("Moved into agreement: baseline differed, rule agrees") is 4,134 — meaning 35 of the 4,169 rows labeled "(the head was misread)" sit under a chain head where Ancestry and FamilySearch do not agree (or that is not a model non-ditto head). Across all 49 states this overstates how many non-target ditto discrepancies come from misreading an agreed chain head and makes the two adjacent tables in Section 3 disagree by `nt_dw_head - nt_d_in`.

**How to check**  
Compare the definitions on lines 203-206 and 214 of MATCHING/ditto_diag.do and the counts on lines 79 and 89 of MATCHING/results/ditto_diag_SMOKE.md (4,169 for nt_dw_head vs. 4,134 for nt_d_in in Wyoming, a 35-record discrepancy that comes entirely from !head_agree). In Stata on wy/merged_data.dta, run `count if nt_dw_head & !head_agree` and `count if nt_d_l_w & !nt_dw_head & prev_namelast!="" & namelast!=prev_namelast & head_anc==""`.

**Suggested fix**  
Require `head_agree` in `nt_dw_head` (or split `nt_dw_head` into head-humans-agree vs. head-humans-disagree/non-ditto-head) so the "(the head was misread)" bucket aligns with `nt_d_in` (`head_agree & namelast==head_anc`), and add `& head_anc!=""` to `nt_dw_chg` on line 204 so records with no Ancestry surname at the head fall into `nt_dw_unk` as stated on line 389.

### 2. Section 3 "All non-target" surname percentages divide `nt_d + nt_nd` numerators by `T_nt` without including `nt_dm` rows

`section3-all-nontarget-surname-denominator-mismatch` · **minor** · correctness · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:185-194, MATCHING/ditto_diag.do:366-373`

**What the code does**  
Lines 366 and 372 compute the "All non-target" column percentages for "Model surname empty (%)" and "Model surname present and different (%)" as `pct `=`T_nt_d_l_e'+`T_nt_nd_l_e'' `T_nt'` and `pct `=`T_nt_d_l_w'+`T_nt_nd_l_w'' `T_nt'`. Any non-target record with missing `non_ditto_ml` (`nt_dm`, defined on line 187) is included in the denominator `T_nt` (and in `nt_diff` on line 194) but excluded from both numerators.

**Why it matters**  
If `non_ditto_ml` is missing on any non-target rows across the 49 states (`T_nt_dm > 0`), the "All non-target" surname empty and surname differing rates in Section 3 are understated relative to `nt_diff` and their true shares in `nt`. (In Wyoming `T_nt_dm == 0`, so the smoke test is unaffected.)

**How to check**  
Inspect MATCHING/ditto_diag.do lines 185-194 and 362-373: if `T_nt_dm > 0` in any of the 49 states, `T_nt_d + T_nt_nd < T_nt`, so the numerators in lines 366 and 372 cover only `nt_d | nt_nd` while the denominator is `T_nt`.

**Suggested fix**  
Either compute `nt_l_e = nt & namelast_u==""` and `nt_l_w = nt & namelast_u!="" & namelast_u!=namelast` directly over `nt` for the "All non-target" column (matching how `nt_f_e`, `nt_f_w`, and `nt_diff` are computed over `nt` on lines 188-194), or assert `assert `T_nt_dm'==0` after the state loop if `non_ditto_ml` is never missing on `nt`.

Duplicate `(roll, image, row)` observations get `dup==1`, and `!dup & !dup[_n-1]` prevents duplicates from either giving or receiving a line-above link.
