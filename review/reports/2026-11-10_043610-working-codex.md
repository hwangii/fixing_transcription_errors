## Findings

### 1. A selected FamilySearch-profile reference sample is treated as ground truth and labeled “Verified Correct”

`selected-reference-labeled-verified-correct` · **MAJOR** · identification · confidence: **likely** (implied, depends on unseen data)

**Where:** `MATCHING/score_fs_links.do:46-59, MATCHING/score_fs_links.do:213-220, MATCHING/score_fs_links.do:376-376, MATCHING/score_fs_links.do:392-395, CODE/create_linked_sample_for_MLP_training.do:5-15`

**What the code does**  
The changed block calls the benchmark a “COMPLETE TRUTH FILE” and hard-codes its revised verified, unverifiable, and contradicted counts. A proposed link is counted as verified when `person_merge == 3 & histid1930 == true_histid1930`, and the output labels that count `Verified Correct`. But the reference dataset is built by inner-merging pre-existing 1930 and 1940 FamilySearch-profile crosswalks and then retaining only profiles with complete, ordered birth and death dates: `keep if birthyr!=. & deathyr!=. & birthmon!=. & deathmon!=. & _merge==3 ...`. Thus the statistic is agreement with a selected genealogical reference, not independently established correctness.

**Why it matters**  
Records must already have been attached to the same FamilySearch profile in both censuses and have complete vital dates to enter the reference set. Attachment is plausibly selective on name legibility, commonness, nativity, and other characteristics related to transcription error; if census names helped construct the upstream crosswalks, the validation is also partly circular. The revised counts therefore cannot by themselves support an unconditional correctness or accuracy claim, and a referee is likely to demand that the selection and independence of the reference standard be established.

**How to check**  
Document how `unique_ID_to_census_crosswalk1930.dta` and `unique_ID_to_census_crosswalk1940.dta` were constructed and whether Ancestry, FamilySearch, or model census names entered those links. Tabulate inclusion in `mlp_training1930_1940.dta` by transcription disagreement, name frequency, race, nativity, literacy, and geography, and independently adjudicate samples currently classified as verified and contradicted.

**Suggested fix**  
Relabel the output as agreement with the genealogical reference rather than `Verified Correct` or truth unless independent validation is supplied. Document the upstream crosswalk construction and reference-sample restrictions, report selection diagnostics, and qualify any extrapolation from this selected benchmark to the full census population.
