### 1. Key table generators include Hawaii despite the paper saying Hawaii is excluded throughout

`hawaii-included-despite-stated-49-jurisdiction-universe` · **MAJOR** · claims-vs-code · confidence: **likely** (implied, depends on unseen data)

**Where:** `CODE/clean_FS_and_combineV5_nber.do:21-25, CODE/clean_FS_and_combineV5_nber.do:186-237, CODE/munir_figures_for_nber.do:223-227, CODE/munir_figures_for_nber.do:232-277, CODE/create_figures_SSHA2025.do:43-47, D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2024-11-18.tex:386-393`

**What the code does**  
Both declared state lists contain `"Hawaii"` and omit Alaska. The construction loop then appends every listed state into `census1940_all_labeled_data.dta`, and the main-results script reads that combined file without dropping Hawaii. The balance-table generator likewise iterates over the Hawaii file. This is a 50-jurisdiction list—48 contiguous states, Hawaii, and DC—not the paper’s stated 49-jurisdiction universe of the contiguous states plus DC.

**Why it matters**  
The reported population, analysis-sample, disagreement, headline linkage, and heterogeneity numbers may include Hawaii while the paper explicitly says Hawaii is excluded and that every table uses the same 49 jurisdictions. The exact numerical effect cannot be measured without the data, but the paper’s sample description and cross-table comparability do not follow from the source.

**How to check**  
Run `tab state_folder_name` immediately after line 43 of `create_figures_SSHA2025.do`, and `tab statefip` immediately before each `balancetable` in `munir_figures_for_nber.do`. Confirm whether state FIPS 15 is present and whether exactly 49 jurisdictions contribute. Recalculate the headline rows after `drop if statefip==15`.

**Suggested fix**  
Define one canonical 49-jurisdiction list excluding Alaska and Hawaii, use it in every construction and analysis script, and assert the contributing state count and absence of FIPS 02 and 15 before writing tables. Then regenerate all affected numbers, or revise the paper to disclose the actual universe.
