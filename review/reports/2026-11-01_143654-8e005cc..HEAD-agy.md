### 1. The paper repeatedly claims improved transcription quality and accuracy without ground truth or adjudicated data

`transcription-quality-claimed-without-ground-truth` · **MAJOR** · claims-vs-code · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:66-71, D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:121-134, D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:257-258, D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:781-797, D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:951-956, CODE/compute_model_agreement_on_discordant.do:123-136, replication/README.md:167`

**What the code does**  
The evaluation code computes exact string agreement between the model and commercial transcriptions (e.g. namefrst==namefrst_ml & namelast==namelast_ml_ditto), or agreement with commercial consensus labels. Table 1 has no producing code. While lines 279, 304-305, and 951 acknowledge that commercial sources are not ground truth, lines 257-258, 901-902, 946, 954, and 955 continue to define and refer to 'sequence accuracy', 'token accuracy', and 'transcription accuracies', and lines 66, 122, 132-134, 781, and 797 claim that ML systematically improves 'transcription quality'.

**Why it matters**  
Referees will challenge claims of 'transcription accuracy' and 'improving transcription quality' when the empirical evidence only demonstrates agreement with commercial sources and downstream linkage increases. Because the paper elsewhere explicitly concedes that commercial sources are not ground truth and that true accuracy would require adjudicated names, the persistent accuracy framing exposes the paper to avoidable attack.

**How to check**  
Run rg -n -i "transcription quality|transcription accur|sequence accuracy|token accuracy" D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex and compare with CODE/compute_model_agreement_on_discordant.do:123-136 and replication/README.md:167.

**Suggested fix**  
Replace accuracy and transcription quality terminology throughout the paper with agreement, consensus reproduction, and linkage improvements. Either restrict accuracy language to an adjudicated ground-truth sample or clearly relabel sequence/token accuracy as sequence/token consensus agreement.

**Why it matters**

**Suggested fix**
