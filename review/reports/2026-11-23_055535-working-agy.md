### 3. Upstream (filename, row) duplicate screen ran on men only for ABE but across men and women for MLP

`upstream-women-added-duplicate-filename-row-asymmetry` · **minor** · identification · confidence: **likely** (implied, depends on unseen data)

**Where:** `MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.do:60-75, MATCHING/1_divide_and_clean_everyoneV5_women_addedV2.do:150-165`

**What the code does**  
`check_mlp_abe_same_input.do` compares `for_mlp1940_tgtns.dta` (built from `divided_file1940_true_cleaned_women_added`) with `divided_file1940_tgtns_cleaned` (built from `divided_file1940_true_cleaned`). In `MATCHING/1_divide_and_clean_everyoneV5_women_addedV2.do:156-165`, duplicate `(filename, row)` observations are dropped to Ancestry fallback via `bysort filename row: gen N=_N` within each birthplace file (`replace namefrst_true_cleaned="" if N>1 & _merge==3`, `gen name_true_cleaned_exists=_merge==3 & N==1`). For ABE (`women_added==""`), `N` counts duplicates among men only; for MLP (`women_added=="_women_added"`), `N` counts duplicates across men and women combined.

**Why it matters**  
If any male record shares a `(filename, row)` with a female record in the same birthplace file `norace<bpl>.dta`, ABE retains his model transcription (`a_ex==1`) while MLP discards it to the Ancestry fallback (`name_true_cleaned_exists==0`). Adding a cross-tabulation of `name_true_cleaned_exists` vs. `a_ex` in `check_mlp_abe_same_input.do` will immediately diagnose whether this upstream asymmetry causes the two inputs to diverge.

**How to check**  
In MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.do, run `tab name_true_cleaned_exists a_ex` on `_merge==3 & sex==1`; any off-diagonal count in `name_true_cleaned_exists==0 & a_ex==1` identifies target men whose `(filename, row)` collided with a female record in `divided_file1940_true_cleaned_women_added`.

**Suggested fix**  
Add `tab name_true_cleaned_exists a_ex` in `MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.do` before `if `ndiff''` so any discrepancy caused by male-female `(filename, row)` collisions in the `_women_added` upstream build is immediately separated from cleaning differences.
