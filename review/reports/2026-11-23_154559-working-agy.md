### 1. Filtering sW to sex==1 before merging on histid misclassifies cross-extract sex changes as men-only-source-only records

`pre-merge-sex-filter-conflates-sex-discrepancies-with-missing-records` · **MAJOR** · correctness · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:43-64`

**What the code does**  
In MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:43-51, inside the `v=="W"` branch (`divided_file<yr>_women_added`), the script runs `keep if sex==1` (line 45) and `drop sex` (line 51) before saving `sW` and running `merge 1:1 histid using `sW'` at line 57. Lines 58-61 then report `count if _merge==1` as `"`yr': men-only source only"` and `count if _merge==2` as `"`yr': women-added source only (men)"`.

**Why it matters**  
Because `sW` is restricted to `sex==1` before the `1:1 histid` merge, every record in `divided_file<yr>` (`sM`, where `sex==1`) that is also present in `divided_file<yr>_women_added` under the same `histid` but coded `sex==2` fails to match and lands in `_merge==1`. From MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.log:389-421, in 1940 only 1,425 men in `divided_file1940` are genuinely absent from `divided_file1940_women_added`, while 4,081 are present in `divided_file1940_women_added` with `sex==2`. Pre-filtering `sW` inflates the 1940 `"men-only source only"` count from 1,425 to 5,506 (74% of which are present in both sources), hides the 1930 cross-extract sex-disagreement count, and prevents checking whether sex recoding between IPUMS extracts 00449/00450 and 00655/00656 also altered gender-specific nickname cleaning in `abeclean ..., sex(sex) initial(mi) nickname` (MATCHING/0_create_file_for_matching_intgenV4_women_added.do:87).

**How to check**  
Compare MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.do:52-67 and MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.log:389-421 (which found 4,081 records with _merge==3 & sex!=1 and 1,425 records with _merge==2 in 1940) against MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:43-61. In extract_diff_diag.do, keeping both sexes in sW through merge 1:1 histid and running `tab _merge sex_W, missing` separates records absent from the women-added extract from records present in both extracts whose sex changed.

**Suggested fix**  
In MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:43-64, do not drop `sex!=1` from `sW` before merging with `sM`. Instead, rename `sex` to `sex_`v''` in both `sM` and `sW`, merge `1:1 histid using `sW'`, and separately count: (1) `_merge==1` (in men-only source, absent from women-added source), (2) `_merge==3 & sex_W!=1` (in both sources by `histid`, male in men-only source but non-male in women-added source), and (3) `_merge==2 & sex_W==1` (male in women-added source, absent from men-only source), before restricting to `keep if _merge==3 & sex_W==1`.

### 2. Diagnostic discards unmatched records without checking shard-list asymmetry from step 0 and does not reconcile d_f against target model-name replacement

`untracked-bpl-shard-asymmetry-and-target-name-overlap` · **minor** · identification · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:20-73, MATCHING/0_create_file_for_matching_intgenV4_women_added.do:144-171`

**What the code does**  
In MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:20-42, `M` (`divided_file<yr>`) and `W` (`divided_file<yr>_women_added`) are loaded by looping independently over `norace*.dta` in each directory without comparing the two birthplace file lists. At lines 58-63, the script prints only the aggregate counts `count if _merge==1` and `count if _merge==2` and immediately executes `keep if _merge==3`, and at lines 65-73 it counts `d_f`, `d_l`, `d_mi`, `d_age`, and `d_bpl` marginally without computing `d_any` or splitting 1940 `d_f` by whether the record's Ancestry name was replaced by a model name in the ABE/MLP inputs.

**Why it matters**  
In MATCHING/0_create_file_for_matching_intgenV4_women_added.do:144-171, lines 146-161 filter in memory (`keep if sex==1` vs. `keep if sex==1|sex==2`, `drop if namefrst_ans_cleaned==""|namelast_ans_cleaned==""`, `keep if bpl<900`, `keep if race==1|race==2`) only to construct `levelsof bpl, local(bpl_list)` (yielding 139 files for 1940 `M` vs. 144 files for 1940 `W`), and line 167 then reloads directly from the unfiltered disk file `matching_intgen<yr><women_added>.dta` using only `if bpl==`b'`. Consequently, birthplace codes that entered `bpl_list` only via women in `W` create `norace<b>.dta` shards in `W` that also pull in any unfiltered men with `bpl==`b'`, while `M` lacks those `norace<b>.dta` shards altogether. Discarding `_merge==1` and `_merge==2` at line 62 without inspecting their `bpl` or empty-name rates, and not cross-tabulating 1940 `d_f` against target model-name replacement, leaves open whether the sample and 61,959 first-name discrepancies between ABE and MLP inputs come purely from the IPUMS extracts or also from step 0's unfiltered reload and step 1's model-name merge.

**How to check**  
In MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do, compare the shard lists `fM` and `fW` via `local onlyW : list fW - fM` (which in 1940 accounts for 144 vs. 139 files per MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.log:362 and MATCHING/1d_for_mlp_tgtns.do:163), tabulate `bpl_M` on `_merge==1` and `bpl_W` on `_merge==2` before `keep if _merge==3`, and merge `tgt_flag1940.dta` (or `name_true_cleaned_exists` from `for_mlp1940_tgtns.dta`) in 1940 to check how many of the `d_f` differences survive on non-target/fallback records vs. being overwritten by model names on target records.

**Suggested fix**  
In MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do: (1) compare the `norace*.dta` file lists between `M` and `W` (`: list fl_M - fl_W` and `: list fl_W - fl_M`) and report `bpl` and empty-name counts on `_merge==1` and `_merge==2` before `keep if _merge==3` at line 62; (2) compute `gen byte d_any = d_f | d_l | d_mi | d_age | d_bpl` at line 70 so total record-level divergence can be compared directly with `d_any: 1,886,605` in MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.log:454; and (3) for 1940, cross-tabulate `d_f` against `tgt` (`tgt_flag1940.dta`) or `name_true_cleaned_exists` to confirm whether raw divided-file `namefrst_ans_cleaned` differences completely explain the 61,959 first-name differences in the final ABE/MLP inputs.

### 3. Examples loop omits age and d_age/d_bpl tabulations silently drop multi-decade age shifts and foreign birthplaces

`omitted-age-examples-and-truncated-age-bpl-tabulations` · **minor** · claims-vs-code · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:10-12, MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:74-85`

**What the code does**  
The header at MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:10-12 promises counts of records differing in first name, surname, initial, age, and birthplace, the distribution of the age difference, and `"examples of each kind"`. However, line 79 loops over `foreach x in f l mi bpl` (omitting `age`); line 75 tabulates `dage` only `if inrange(dage,-12,12)` (where `, missing` never displays missing or out-of-range values because `inrange(dage,-12,12)` is false for `|dage|>12` and `.`); and line 78 tabulates `bpl_M bpl_W` only `if d_bpl & bpl_M<=56 & bpl_W<=56` without counting how many `d_bpl` records involve foreign birthplaces (`56 < bpl < 900`).

**Why it matters**  
In MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.log:469-797, 3 of the first 21 age-difference examples are multi-decade digit differences outside `[-12, 12]` (`54 vs 34`, `61 vs 7`, `60 vs 40`), and 3 of the first 9 birthplace-difference examples involve foreign IPUMS birthplace codes `> 56` (`18 vs 414` Indiana/Ireland, `25 vs 420` Massachusetts/Belgium, `414 vs 411` Ireland/Scotland). Because both ABE and MLP block on exact `bpl` (`bpl < 900`) and age windows, excluding `|dage| > 12` and `bpl > 56` without counting their shares omits a substantial portion of the linking-relevant discrepancies.

**How to check**  
Inspect MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:10-12 and 74-85, and compare against the first 30 discrepant records in MATCHING/check_mlp_abe_same_input.log:469-797, where 3 of 21 age-discrepant examples have `|dage| >= 20` (lines 664, 697, 763) and 3 of 9 birthplace-discrepant examples involve foreign birthplace codes `bpl > 56` (`414`, `420`, `411` at lines 576, 741, 796).

**Suggested fix**  
In MATCHING/extract_diff_diag.do:74-85: (1) include `age` in the examples loop at line 79 (`foreach x in f l mi age bpl`); (2) alongside `tab dage if inrange(dage,-12,12)`, report `count if d_age & !inrange(dage,-12,12)` (and `count if d_age & mod(abs(dage),10)==0` to capture 10/20/30-year digit shifts); and (3) before `tab bpl_M bpl_W if d_bpl & bpl_M<=56 & bpl_W<=56`, count how many `d_bpl` records have `bpl_M<=56 & bpl_W<=56` (US-vs-US), `(bpl_M<=56) != (bpl_W<=56)` (US-vs-foreign), and `bpl_M>56 & bpl_W>56` (foreign-vs-foreign).
