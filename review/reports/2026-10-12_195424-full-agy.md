## Findings

### 1. Validation of linked pairs against genealogical links is presented as proof of transcription accuracy

`link-validation-presented-as-transcription-accuracy` · **MAJOR** · claims-vs-code · confidence: **verified** (traced in source)

**Where:** `D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:70, D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:104-106, D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:565-573, D:/repos/6638fa0fd89fbec05130caeb/DHTS-2026-10-12.tex:648-650`

**What the code does**  
The paper's external validation exercise in Section 3.2 classifies census-to-census linked pairs against FamilySearch genealogical pairs on 1940 record IDs. It categorizes pairs as verified correct, directly contradicted, or unverifiable depending on whether the genealogical source links to the same 1930 partner, a different 1930 partner, or is missing from FamilySearch. No name string is evaluated against ground truth. Indeed, Section 3.3 (lines 648-650) explicitly acknowledges: 'Section 3.2 validates the links our transcriptions produce against an external genealogical standard. A second and distinct exercise, which we describe here and have not yet completed, validates the transcriptions themselves: rather than asking whether a link is right, it asks how close each transcription comes to the name a genealogical profile records.'

**Why it matters**  
The Abstract (line 70) and Introduction (lines 104-106) assert that genealogical validation confirms 'genuine accuracy improvements rather than spurious matches.' In reality, link validation cannot evaluate transcription accuracy, and over half of newly added exact-name links on discordant records (1,460,694 of 2,804,662, or 52.1% in Table 6/deaglan_abe_jw_restricted.tex) are unverifiable. A referee will reject the claim that genealogical link recovery establishes transcription accuracy or rules out false-positive matches.

**How to check**  
Compare lines 70 and 104-106 with lines 648-650 in DHTS-2026-10-12.tex. Inspect figures/deaglan_abe_jw_restricted.tex:16 to confirm that 1,460,694 of 2,804,662 new exact-name links are unverifiable under the FamilySearch reference set.

**Suggested fix**  
In the Abstract and Introduction, soften claims from 'genuine accuracy improvements' and ruling out spurious matches to the precise result: increased recovery of verified links and lower contradicted link shares within an incomplete reference standard. Clarify that validating name transcriptions directly against profile names is pending as described in Section 3.3.
